## Purpose and status

This file is raw/reference material, in the same spirit as
`sample_pool_workout_traditional.md` and
`sample_pool_workout_openwater_focus.md` (verbatim workout text, not
evidence-tagged, not a source for physiological claims) — **with one
important difference from those two files: every workout below has a real,
independently fetched, currently-live web source**, unlike the two sample
files, which are themselves explicitly "never citable sources" per
`14-swim-set-structure.md`. This file exists to (1) feed a future
data-driven workout-template library, and (2) stress-test whether
`14-swim-set-structure.md`'s six-shape main-set taxonomy actually covers
what real masters coaches publish.

Every source URL below was fetched directly (via WebFetch) during
research for this file and confirmed to contain the quoted text at fetch
time (late July 2026). Per `00-conventions.md`'s citation-integrity rule
and the fabrication incident documented in that file's header: **do not
add an entry to this file without independently fetching and confirming
the source** — no URL, author, or team name here should ever be invented.

## Format-taxonomy reference

Classifications below refer to `14-swim-set-structure.md`'s six-shape
main-set menu:
1. straight-continuous-reps
2. broken-distance-lite
3. descend
4. pyramid
5. broken-distance ladder (paired, e.g. 100+300, 200+200, 300+100)
6. negative-split

Where a workout's main set doesn't cleanly match any of the six, it is
flagged **novel** with a short reason — those are flagged as the most
useful output of this pass, per this task's brief.

## 1. "Who's in the Pool?" — Level A

**Source:** Sara McLarty, *Masters and Triathlon Swimming Workouts by Sara
McLarty* (blog), post "Who's in the Pool?", June 28, 2021.
https://mastersswimworkoutsbysaramclarty.blogspot.com/2021/06/whos-in-pool.html

**Verbatim text (Level A):**
> 600 warm up (100 swim/50 kick/repeat).
> 3 x 200 swim @ 3:00-3:20 (descend 1-3).
> 400 swim (No Walls, turn at the "T").
> 3 x 200 swim @ 2:50-3:10 (descend 1-3).
> 400 swim (No Walls, turn at the "T").
> 3 x 200 with paddles @ 2:40-3:00 (descend 1-3).
> 200 cool down.

**Distance:** not stated as a single total in the post itself; summing the
listed reps gives roughly 3,400 yd/m.

**Classification: descend.** Each of the three `3 x 200` blocks is
explicitly labeled "descend 1-3" — a clean, repeated instance of the
taxonomy's descend shape, run three times with a continuous "no-walls" 400
as a breather between blocks.

## 2. "Let's Swim" — Level A

**Source:** Sara McLarty, same blog, post "Let's Swim", June 7, 2021.
https://mastersswimworkoutsbysaramclarty.blogspot.com/2021/06/lets-swim.html

**Verbatim text (Level A):**
> 300 fins/300 no fins. 250 pull/250 swim. 200 IM/200 swim. 150 kick/150
> swim. 100 with sighting/100 swim. 6 x 200 w/:20 rest (odds: no walls and
> FAST; evens: regular swim and easy). 300 cool down (50 kick/50 swim).
> *3500 total*

**Distance:** 3,500 yd/m (stated).

**Classification: novel — two distinct shapes, neither in the taxonomy.**
1. The opening section is a monotonically **descending distance ladder of
   paired variants** (300→250→200→150→100, each rung paired with a
   technique variant of the same length) — this is a one-directional
   ladder, not the taxonomy's up-then-down pyramid, and not the paired
   summing-ladder example (`100+300, 200+200, 300+100`) either, since here
   every rung is the *same* length repeated twice, not two different
   lengths.
2. The `6 x 200` block alternates hard/easy by odd/even rep
   (odds no-walls-and-fast, evens regular-and-easy) with no across-set
   progression — an **alternating-intensity repeat** shape the taxonomy
   doesn't name (it isn't descend, since there's no progression, and isn't
   negative-split, since the alternation is between whole reps, not within
   one rep).

## 3. "Build to Fast (LCM)" — Level A

**Source:** Sara McLarty, same blog, post "Build to Fast (LCM)", June 4,
2021.
https://mastersswimworkoutsbysaramclarty.blogspot.com/2021/06/build-to-fast-lcm.html

**Verbatim text (Level A):**
> 2 x 200 swim/100 kick for warm up.
> 400 (25 right hand in fist, 25 left hand in fist, 50 build to fast).
> 400 (50 breathing every 5, 50 build to fast).
> 400 IM (25 kick, 25 drill, 25 stroke, 25 free build to fast).
> 400, 300, 200, 100 pull, w/:20 rest (last 100 of each is build to fast).
> 400 with paddles only (25 right arm only, 25 left arm only, 50 build to
> fast).
> 400 cool down with kick board (50 kick, 50 sail boat drill).
> *3600 total*

**Distance:** 3,600 yd/m (stated).

**Classification: novel — repeated "build-finish" reps.** Every main-set
rep (mostly uniform 400s, plus one 400→300→200→100 descending-distance
pull ladder) ends in an explicit "build to fast" segment. The defining
shape here is a progressive-acceleration tail baked into *every single
rep*, repeated rep after rep — distinct from negative-split (a discrete
first-half/second-half split) and from descend (pace changes *across*
reps, not within each one). The embedded 400-300-200-100 pull ladder is
itself a second, smaller instance of the same "descending-distance ladder"
novel shape seen in workout #2 above.

## 4. Swim Dojo — "Feb 4A"

**Source:** Swim Dojo, "Masters Workout" archive page, workout dated
"Feb 4A". https://www.swimdojo.com/masters-workout

**Verbatim text:**
> **Warm Up** — 6×50 (odds swim, evens kick/drill) *(300)*
>
> **Main set** — 2x thru: 2×100 @ b+15 one IM/one Free; 4×50 @ b+10 2
> Stroke/2 Free; 8×25 FAST/EZ @ :30/:35/:40/:45 — round 1 Kick, round 2
> Swim; 4×50 @ b+10/+5/0/-5; 50 EZ @ b+20; 2×100 one on +5/-5; 50 EZ
> *(2400)*

**Distance:** stated subtotals sum to 300 (warm-up) + 2,400 (main set) =
2,700 yd/m; no separate overall total given on the page.

**Classification: novel (compound, multi-shape).** The `4×50 @
b+10/+5/0/-5` segment alone is a textbook **descend** (interval tightens
each rep, same distance). But the main set as a whole interleaves that
descend block with an alternating FAST/EZ 25s block (round 1 kick, round 2
swim) and a variable-differential 100 pair — a compound structure that
isn't reducible to any single one of the six shapes.

## 5. Swim Dojo — "Feb 4B"

**Source:** Swim Dojo, same archive page, workout dated "Feb 4B".
https://www.swimdojo.com/masters-workout

**Verbatim text:**
> **Warm Up** — 200 swim; 4×50 Choice, build to fast; 4×25 Choice, variable
> sprint *(500)*
>
> **Main Set** — 6×25 Free, ≤3 breaths max @ :30/:35/:40; 4×25 Kick on Back
> @ :35/:40/:45; 100 Free, smooth; ~ 1×75 Free, FAST for time; 1×175 Free,
> recovery
>
> **Warm Down** — 75 swim; 50 drill; 25 stroke; 50 drill; 75 free; 25 easy
> to get to right end of pool
>
> **TOTAL: 2600**

**Distance:** 2,600 yd/m (stated).

**Classification: novel.** The `1×75 FAST-for-time` immediately followed
by `1×175 recovery` is a single hard effort paired with one longer, easy
recovery-distance swim — not a repeated set at all, so it doesn't fit
straight-reps/descend/pyramid; not a negative split (that's one rep,
internally paced fast-then-faster or even-paced, not a maximal effort
followed by a separate easy distance); and not broken-distance-lite either,
since that shape is defined as *aerobic/easy character* throughout, while
here the first segment is a maximal, not-easy effort. The 6×25 hypoxic
block (progressively less rest, same distance) is a milder, arguably-legal
instance of descend, but the file logs the set as a whole under novel
because the paired hard/easy couplet is its dominant, distinguishing
feature.

## 6–10. USMS, "Best Workouts for Masters Swimmers"

**Source:** Kris Goodrich, U.S. Masters Swimming, May 5, 2021.
https://www.usms.org/fitness-and-training/articles-and-videos/articles/best-workouts-for-masters-swimmers

### 6. Workout 1 — Pace Workouts (3,300 yd, stated)
Main set: `5×100 @ 500-pace` → `500 continuous @ same pace` → `4×100 @
500-pace` → `400 continuous` → `3×100 @ 500-pace` → `300 continuous`.

**Classification: novel.** Alternates straight repeat-blocks with
continuous swims at a constant *target pace*, while both the repeat count
and the continuous distance shrink each round — the defining variable
here is distance/count, not speed (pace target is flat throughout), so
this doesn't match descend, and it isn't paired-summing ladder either.

### 7. Workout 2 — Ultra-Short Race-Pace Training (3,300 yd, stated)
Main set: `20×50 @ target-200-pace+20s rest` → 300 break → `20×25
alternating stroke/free @ target-100-pace+20s rest`.

**Classification: straight-continuous-reps.** Uniform reps, one constant
target pace per block — a clean match.

### 8. Workout 3 — Test Sets (3,000 yd, stated)
Main set: `6×150` IM-rotation (continuing the IM order rep to rep: 50 of
each stroke, picking up where the prior rep left off) + `10×100 freestyle`.

**Classification:** the `10×100` block is **straight-continuous-reps**.
The `6×150` IM-rotation block is **novel** — a stroke-order rotation
carried forward across reps is an organizing principle the taxonomy (which
is entirely about pace/distance shape) doesn't address at all.

### 9. Workout 4 — Breathing Workout (2,550 yd, stated)
Main set (each block separated by 100 easy): `2×25 free` → `4×25 breast` →
`6×25 back` → `8×25 fly` → `6×25 back` → `4×25 breast` → `2×25 free`, each
block with a breathing-pattern constraint.

**Classification: pyramid.** A textbook count-based pyramid (2-4-6-8-6-4-2)
mirrored around a peak — one of the cleanest taxonomy matches in this
whole set.

### 10. Workout 5 — Recovery (3,100 yd, stated)
Main set: 4 rounds of `[2×25 kick, 2×50 drill, 2×100 swim, 2:00 rest]`,
then a single `500` swum as an ascending in-line ladder: `25 free/25
stroke, 50 free/50 stroke, 75 free/75 stroke, 100 free/100 stroke`.

**Classification: novel.** The closing 500 is a continuous, single-rep,
ascending free/stroke-pair ladder (25→50→75→100, both halves growing
together within one unbroken swim) — distinct from the taxonomy's
broken-distance ladder, which describes separate paired *reps* of
differing length, not one continuous swim that ascends internally.

## 11–15. USMS, "Best 3,000-Yard Swimming Workouts"

**Source:** Kris Goodrich, U.S. Masters Swimming, January 2, 2024.
https://www.usms.org/fitness-and-training/articles-and-videos/articles/best-3000-yard-swimming-workouts

### 11. Workout 1 — All About Those Legs (3,050 yd, stated)
Main set: `2×200 kick` → `4×100 kick` → `8×50 kick` → `16×25 kick`, each
block same sprint-character effort.

**Classification: novel.** A halving-distance/doubling-count kick ladder
across the whole main set (200→100→50→25 while rep count doubles each
time) — not the taxonomy's paired ladder, and not a pyramid (no return
leg back up).

### 12. Workout 2 — Stroke Work (2,900 yd, stated)
Main set: 3 rounds, each round assigned a different stroke focus, of
`4×75` + `3×50` + `2×75 sprint`.

**Classification: novel — stroke-rotation-by-round.** Each of the three
rounds carries a distinct stroke emphasis; this is exactly the kind of
"stroke-focus set" flagged as a plausible new format type in this task's
brief.

### 13. Workout 3 — Freestyle Focus (3,000 yd, stated)
Main set: 3x through `[200 smooth, 4×50 kick-swim, 3×100 descend 1-3]`.

**Classification: descend.** The repeated `3×100 descend 1-3` block,
run three times, is the dominant, explicitly-labeled shape.

### 14. Workout 4 — Pull Set (2,900 yd, stated)
Main set: `4×75` → `300 pull` → `4×75` (tighter interval) → `300 pull` →
`4×75` (tighter still) → `300 pull` → `4×75`, with the `4×75` interval
tightening 1:30 → 1:25 → 1:20 → 1:15 across the repeating blocks, while
the interleaved `300 pull` swims stay constant.

**Classification: novel.** An interval-tightening block-descend
(progressively less rest, same distance, across *repeating blocks*)
interleaved with fixed continuous efforts — close in spirit to descend but
operating at the level of repeated sub-sets with a constant swim
interleaved, not the taxonomy's single-set same-distance descend.

### 15. Workout 5 — Fast With Some Breath Control (2,950 yd, stated)
Main set, per stroke (fly → back → breast → free): `4×50 fast with
descending breath count` → `4×25 sprint, half underwater` → `200 easy`,
repeated once per stroke.

**Classification: novel.** Combines the stroke-rotation shape (#12) with
an internal descend (breathing constraint tightens within each stroke's
block) — a compound shape, not one bucket.

## 16. MySwimPro — "Beast Training Workout"

**Source:** MySwimPro blog, "Beast Training Workout", June 13, 2019.
https://blog.myswimpro.com/2019/06/13/beast-training-workout/

**Verbatim structure:**
> Main Set — 4×100 Freestyle @1:30 (no paddles, pull buoy), averaged 1:09;
> 4×100 Freestyle @1:25 (small paddles, pull buoy), averaged 1:03; 4×100
> Freestyle @1:20 (large paddles, pull buoy), averaged 1:01. Final Set —
> 300 Freestyle Best Average (paddles, no pull buoy), averaged 1:02.

**Distance/duration:** 3,000 yd total, 59 min (both stated).

**Classification: descend.** Same distance (100s) each block, progressively
faster average pace and tighter interval block-to-block — a clean descend
match. Note: the paddle-size progression (none → small → large) riding
alongside the pace progression is a secondary, taxonomy-unaddressed detail
worth flagging for template design, even though the core shape is descend.

## 17–21. USMS, "Best Sprint Workouts for Swimming Faster"

**Source:** Matt Donovan, U.S. Masters Swimming, May 5, 2021.
https://www.usms.org/fitness-and-training/articles-and-videos/articles/best-sprint-workouts-for-swimming-faster

### 17. Workout 1 — Breathing Pattern (2,150 yd, stated)
Main set: a descending-distance ladder (`400 pull` → `300 free` → `200
free` → `100 free`, each at "moderate effort" with a breathing-pattern
constraint), with a fixed `8×25 @ :10 rest` filler block repeated between
every rung.

**Classification: novel.** Same family as workouts #2, #3, and #11's
one-directional descending-distance ladders — here reinforced by a fixed
filler set between rungs. Recurring enough across this research pass to
suggest "descending-rung ladder with fixed filler reps" is a real,
independently-recurring format worth adding to the taxonomy.

### 18. Workout 2 — Repeat Sprints (2,100 yd, stated)
Main set: 10 identical rounds of `[50 fast+fins, 2×25 fast+fins, 50
recovery back]`, 1:00 rest between rounds, no progression round to round.

**Classification: straight-continuous-reps.** Uniform compound "rounds"
repeated unchanged — the closest fit even though each "rep" is itself a
three-piece compound, because the across-round shape is flat/uniform.

### 19. Workout 3 — Kick and Swim Sprints (2,600 yd, stated)
Main set: 3 identical rounds of `[6×50 free, 200 kick, 100 recovery
back]`, no progression.

**Classification: straight-continuous-reps.**

### 20. Workout 4 — Descend to Sprint (2,500 yd, stated)
Main set: 2 rounds of `[3×100 free descend by 100, 3×100 pull descend by
100, 3×100 kick descend by 100, 50 recovery]`.

**Classification: descend** (explicitly labeled "descend by 100," applied
across three modalities per round).

### 21. Workout 5 — Broken 200 (2,600 yd, stated)
Main set: 6 identical rounds with fins of `[100 free fast, 2×50 free fast,
100 recovery back]`, no progression round to round.

**Classification: straight-continuous-reps.**

- **novel:** 12 (#2, #3, #4, #5, #6, #8, #10, #11, #12, #14, #15, #17)

**Recurring novel shapes worth naming as real candidate format types:**
1. **One-directional descending (or ascending) distance ladder** — a
   single monotonic ladder of rep lengths (e.g. 400-300-200-100, or
   200-100-50-25), as opposed to the taxonomy's *paired* ladder
   (100+300, 200+200, 300+100). Appeared 4 times (#2, #3, #11, #17) —
   this is the single most common novel shape found and the strongest
   candidate for a genuinely new taxonomy entry.
2. **Stroke-rotation-by-round/rep** — assigning a different stroke focus
   to each round or rep in a set, independent of pace/distance shape.
   Appeared in #8 (IM rotation) and #12/#15 (stroke-by-round).
3. **Alternating-intensity reps** (odd/even hard-easy, not progressive) —
   #2's 6×200.
4. **Repeated build-finish reps** — every rep, not just one, ends in a
   progressive-acceleration tail. #3.
5. **Single-rep hard/easy couplet** (one maximal effort + one paired easy
   recovery-distance swim, not a repeated set) — #5.
6. **Compound multi-block main sets** that interleave two or more of the
   above/existing shapes in one set and resist a single classification —
   #4, #14, #15.
